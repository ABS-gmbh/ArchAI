# Gentium Plus

The face for transcriptions (`--font-gentium`, the `font-text` utility).

- **Version:** 6.200
- **Source:** SIL International's release,
  https://github.com/silnrsi/font-gentium/releases/download/v6.200/GentiumPlus-6.200.zip
  (also at https://software.sil.org/gentium/). The files are `web/GentiumPlus-*.woff2`
  from that archive, unchanged.
- **Licence:** SIL Open Font License 1.1, in [`OFL.txt`](OFL.txt).

These are the complete fonts, not a subset. That matters: Google Fonts serves
Gentium Plus split by script, and none of its subsets includes the medieval
combining letters (U+0363–U+036F, the superscript a in mͣ and its kind),
which a browser then draws in another face. These files have them, along with
Latin Extended-D (ꝰ ꝑ ꝓ ꝯ) and the Tironian et (⁊).

They do not have U+1DD3 or U+1DE4 (Combining Diacritical Marks Supplement);
no Gentium Plus release does.
