# Pivot To Semantic Extraction

The canonical thesis showcase pages are grammatical, prognostic, and doctrinal witnesses rather than entity-rich historical narratives.
Forcing named-entity authority linking here would reward false positives rather than useful scholarship.
The honest thesis move is therefore to present Figure 30 as semantic extraction / mention-handling behavior:

- grammatical and pedagogical terms on the Old English page,
- abstract moral and theological concepts on the Old French page,
- prognostic and moral-practical concepts on the Latin Abaton page,
- and the system's ability to suppress authority linking when those mentions are not genuine linkable entities.
